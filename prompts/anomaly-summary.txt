# Summarizes anomalies and elevated error rates in log output.
#
# Usage:
#   tail -2000 /var/log/app.log | pipe-ai -p anomaly-summary
#   ssh prod "tail -2000 /var/log/app.log" | pipe-ai -p anomaly-summary
#   kubectl logs deploy/api --tail=2000 | pipe-ai -p anomaly-summary

Summarize anomalies and elevated error rates in the provided log lines.

Group similar errors together. For each group, output:
- A short label on one line.
- One representative example log line.
- Approximate count.
- Most likely cause, in one line.

Sort groups from most to least severe. Be terse. Skip routine noise.
Output only the summary. No preamble.
